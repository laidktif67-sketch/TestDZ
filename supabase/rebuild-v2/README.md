# Rebuild v2 candidate scripts

These scripts describe the isolated TestDZ rebuild candidate.

- `001_schema.sql`: candidate tables, indexes, RLS enablement/force.
- `002_security.sql`: private security functions, triggers, policies and grants.
- `003_app_lifecycle.sql`: developer app quota/create/update/archive/restore RPCs with invoker wrappers and private definer implementations.
- `004_identity_sessions.sql`: validated onboarding plus self-only active-session and security-activity RPCs.
- `005_support_notifications.sql`: ticket creation/replies, attachment metadata registration, and owner-scoped notification read-state RPCs.
- `006_storage_policies.sql`: five preserved bucket definitions, owner/participant upload and read policies, and private evidence access helpers.
- `007_tester_workflow.sql`: tester discovery, joining, assignments, check-ins, feedback, submissions, and private evidence workflow.
- `008_developer_lifecycle.sql`: app submission/admin review, test create/update/submission/admin review, and developer cohort creation RPCs.
- `009_lock_app_edits_during_review.sql`: blocks developer edits while an app is pending review or published; edits are limited to draft/rejected states.
- `010_guard_test_cohort_capacity.sql`: requires a published parent app and cohort capacity at least equal to the test's minimum tester target.
- `011_test_content_authoring.sql`: owner-checked atomic save of ordered test steps and requirements, editable only while the test is draft/failed/cancelled; no direct browser table writes.
- `012_cohort_management.sql`: owner-only forming-cohort edits and audited lifecycle transitions; pending assignments are withdrawn on safe cancellation, and cohort readiness requires the test's minimum tester count.
- `013_private_test_builds.sql`: private APK bucket, owner-registered build metadata, upload verification, tester-participant read policy, and APK-test review gate.
- `014_harden_test_build_storage_helper.sql`: narrows Storage upload authorization to the exact pending metadata path registered by the owning developer.
- `015_private_test_media_assets.sql`: private developer-supplied images, videos and documents with owner registration, MIME/size checks, verified upload finalization and assigned-tester read access.
- `016_private_developer_feedback.sql`: owner-scoped developer feedback RPCs, sanitized tester labels, and removal of developer access to raw assignments, check-ins and feedback.

**Important:** This folder is deliberately not `supabase/migrations`. Do not apply it to production or move it into the active migration path until the validation gates in [the rebuild plan](../../docs/database-rebuild-v2.md) pass.

Apply in order: `001_schema.sql`, `002_security.sql`, `003_app_lifecycle.sql`, `004_identity_sessions.sql`, `005_support_notifications.sql`, and `006_storage_policies.sql`, then `007_tester_workflow.sql`, `008_developer_lifecycle.sql`, `009_lock_app_edits_during_review.sql`, `010_guard_test_cohort_capacity.sql`, `011_test_content_authoring.sql`, `012_cohort_management.sql`, `013_private_test_builds.sql`, and `014_harden_test_build_storage_helper.sql`, `015_private_test_media_assets.sql`, and `016_private_developer_feedback.sql`. Run `tests/security_contract.sql`, `tests/003_app_lifecycle_contract.sql`, `tests/004_profile_contract.sql`, `tests/005_developer_lifecycle_contract.sql`, `tests/006_test_media_assets_contract.sql`, `tests/007_tester_workflow_contract.sql`, and `tests/008_developer_feedback_privacy_contract.sql` after all scripts. The developer lifecycle contract also verifies non-owner denial, malformed content rejection, content lock after submission, audit logging, and rollback cleanup for test steps and requirements. The app lifecycle contract validates authenticated developer app create/read/update/archive/restore, quota enforcement, ownership isolation, and app-icon Storage helper authorization. The profile contract validates self-only profile reads/updates and protected verification/ban flags. All use rolled-back transactions. The developer lifecycle contract validates app submission and admin-only approval, test creation/submission and admin-only approval, test content authoring, cohort creation/update/cancellation, minimum tester readiness, private APK registration/upload verification/participant download, tester join, and audit-event creation. Validated in TestDZ-FixLab by applying candidate scripts through `010_guard_test_cohort_capacity.sql` to `rebuild_v2` and replaying them into temporary `rebuild_v2_replay` / `rebuild_v2_private_replay` schemas, with replay-only Storage policy names. The Auth signup trigger was omitted only from replay to avoid attaching a second trigger to shared `auth.users`; it remains present in the actual candidate script. Replay schemas and policies were removed after verification. Scripts 011 through 014 were then applied to the actual FixLab candidate schema and exercised by the rolled-back developer lifecycle contract, including an authenticated Storage INSERT-policy check for private APKs.

The private test-media contract validates registered upload authorization, MIME/size rejection, assigned-tester reads, unassigned-tester denial, and rollback cleanup. The developer feedback privacy contract validates that developers cannot read raw tester assignments, check-ins, feedback rows or internal message/review tables; the owner-only RPC returns feedback without tester identifiers, and review/message actions are isolated to tests owned by the developer.
